# About me

- 🔭 I’m currently based in Aix-en-Provence, France, where I'm working on the project L2-SPELL: The impact of orthography on second language word learning and processing together with Dr. Pauline Welby in the Laboratoire Parole Langage (Aix-Marseille Université, CNRS)
- 🌱 In January 2026, I received my PhD project in French linguistics at Lund University, Sweden, after having successfully defended my thesis in which I studied the interpretation and processing of gender-fair forms in French .
